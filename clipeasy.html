ClipEasy Video-Editing Software Prototype


FigmaHTMLCSSJavaScriptClaude A.I.ChatGPT

Role

User Researcher and Secondary Designer

About

This video-editing software prototype was created as a academci project, in collaboration with 3 other group members, to explore the design and development of a user-friendly desktop video-editing application. The goal was to create an intuitive interface that allows users to easily edit videos, add effects, and export their projects. The project involved conducting user research to understand the needs and preferences of potential users, as well as designing wireframes and mockups with Figma to visualize the final product. With the assistance of Claude A.I. and ChatGPT, we were able to generate prototypes forthe software. The final prototype was developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and was tested with users to gather feedback and make improvements.
