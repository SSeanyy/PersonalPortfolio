Brock University Student Portal Prototype

About

Our task for this academic project was to take any website of our choice and redesign it however we'd like. For this prototype, I decided to redesign Brock University's student self-serve account. The current self-serve portal looks old and outdated and can be confusing to navigate, especially for newer students. My goal with this redesign was to not only make it look better, but also make it easier to navigate.
