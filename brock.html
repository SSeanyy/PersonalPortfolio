Brock University Student Portal Prototype


FigmaHTMLCSS

The Problem

Students were tasked to redesign a exisitng website we use on a daily basis, and redesign it in a way you think would improve the current design and user experience. For this project, I decided to redesign Brock University's student self-serve account. The current self-serve portal looks old and outdated and can be confusing to navigate, especially for newer students.

Upon entering the homepage, users are immediately greeted with an abundance of text and different sections that can ultimately overwhelm many individuals. In addition, the typography, colours, and layout used throughout the website all contribute to its outdated look and feel. For wesbites like this, students and faculty shouldn't have to look where specific forms are for 10 minutes straight. It should be intuitive and easy to navigate.

There is way too much. SIMPLICITY IS KEY!

The Solution

My goal with this redesign was to not only make it look better, but to also make it intuitive and easier to navigate. I decided to stick to only 2 colours to maintain a consistent colour palette all throughout the website. I believe colour establishes identity, and with this, the colours used here still maintains Brock University's visual identity.

The font used on the current wesbite is rather small and cramped together making it hard to read, and when the time comes to look for something, there are no indicators that assist you in finding it. My solution was to break up chunks of the text and sort them into corresponding sections that can be easy to follow and understand. Oh! want to find a form? Look in the forms section! Can't find your courses Look in the courses section! All of the decision choices seen thorughout my redesign were used to in order to improve design, user sastisfaction and experience.
