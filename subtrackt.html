🟡SubTrackt - Subscription Tracker Prototype


HTMLCSSJavaScript

The Problem

The goal of this project was to solve a real-world problem that people face daily and develop a website to address it. The problem chosen was online subscriptions. With so many digital services in our lives today (Amazon, Netflix, Apple TV+, and more), it's hard to keep track of them all. Let’s be honest, almost everyone has forgotten to cancel a subscription a time or two, especially with all these “Free for (X) months, then pay (Y) after!” deals. These offers seem to be attractive and convenient at first, but when the charges start appearing unexpectedly, they quickly become frustrating.

The Solution

SubTrackt solves this by letting users manage all of their subscriptions in one place, with reminders a day, week, or even a month before the next renewal date, so they're never caught off guard. With SubTrackt’s easy-to-navigate, user-friendly, and engaging platform, it provides an ease-of-mind experience when dealing with your subscriptions by allowing users to directly manage every subscription directly within their personalized dashboard, giving them an overview of where their money is going. In addition, SubTrackt avoids these surprises through personalized reminders where users can choose to be notified a day, a week, or even a month before the renewal date to ensure that the user still wishes to continue with their subscription. These alerts help prevent surprise charges and keep you in control. With SubTrackt, managing subscriptions becomes simple, organized, and stress-free.
