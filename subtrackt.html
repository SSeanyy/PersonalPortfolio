SubTrackt — Subscription Tracker Prototype


HTMLCSSJavaScript

About

The goal of this project was to solve a real-world problem that people face daily and develop a website to address it. The problem chosen was online subscriptions — with so many digital services in our lives today (Amazon, Netflix, Apple TV+, and more), it's hard to keep track of them all. SubTrackt solves this by letting users manage all of their subscriptions in one place, with reminders a day, week, or even a month before the next renewal date, so they're never caught off guard.
