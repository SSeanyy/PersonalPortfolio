PeekABoo — Capstone Project


AudacityLMMSPianoGuitar

Role

Lead Sound Engineer and Foley Artist

About

This project served as a capstone project in the Interactive Arts and Science (IASC) program. Our team chose to develop a video-game: "PeekABoo". It is a single-player psychological puzzle game where solving mini-games becomes the key to finding missing children at a daycare when the power goes out. My role as the lead Sound Engineer and Foley Artist within this project was being responsible for developing all of the background tracks and environmental sound effects heard throughout the game.
